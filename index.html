Our games

Scrattle

Logic word puzzles, daily

Every row and column gives you its letters, jumbled. Work out the words, cross-check them, and fill the grid. Every puzzle has exactly one answer, so you never need to guess.

Three sizes

  • Mini 5×5
  • Classic 7×7
  • Big 9×9

Gem Buzz

Stack, fuse and upgrade gems

Drop gem pieces on a honeycomb board, stack matching colors and fuse them into blasts that set off chain reactions. Fuse over a gem again and it grows.

Gem tiers

  • Shard
  • Gem
  • Jewel
  • Crown
  • Heart

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Hexcape

Hex sliding puzzle

A small stretchy alien is stuck on a derelict hex space station. Slide asteroids, cargo pods and crystal shards out of the way to open a path to the airlock.

Two modes

  • Slide
  • Snake

How we make games

  • No account needed

    Open the game and play. Your progress stays on your phone, and every game works offline.

  • Ads you choose

    Videos that give you something are always optional. Other ads wait for a natural pause and never interrupt a puzzle.

  • One purchase, no ads

    Each game has a single, one-time Remove Ads purchase. No subscriptions.

  • Made for everyone

    Colorblind symbols, reduced-motion and haptics settings, and controls that work with one thumb.
